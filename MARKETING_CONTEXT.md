## 7. AI Writing Scrubber (Tone Humanization)
This section defines structural checks to eliminate standard AI writing footprints and ensure natural, human-like cadence.

- **Banned AI Vocabulary:** The writing agent is strictly forbidden from using: *"delve", "unlock", "game-changing", "tapestry", "testament", "moreover", "demystify", "realm", "revolutionize", "beacon", "pinnacle"*.
- **Burstiness Checklist:**
  - Alternating sentence lengths (e.g., follow a 20-word descriptive sentence with an 8-word punchy sentence).
  - Paragraphs must be kept under 3 lines (max 4 sentences) to facilitate reading scan paths.
- **Conversational Tone:** Inject direct address ("you", "your") and natural contractions (*"don't", "can't", "we've", "it's"*).
- **EEAT Validation:** Inject at least one specific real-world example, case study reference, or a contrarian perspective that challenges industry assumptions.
